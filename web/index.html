Building Reliable Outcome

A voice teammate for your code.

Say “Hey bro”, talk through what you are building, and get straight answers about your project. It reads your code, plans the next step with you, and asks before changing anything.

Open the app See the code on GitHub

Free. Open source. Works in Chrome and Edge, on desktop and phone.

Built to feel like yours

Hands-free voice

Say “Hey bro” and keep your hands on the keyboard. Follow-up questions need no wake word for a while.

Talks like a teammate

Short, direct and focused on the same goal as you: finishing the project. Coding only, no tangents.

Private by default

No account and no B.R.O. server. Chats stay on your device, secrets are stripped before saving, and keys can be locked with a passphrase.

Bring your own brain

Use Claude, OpenAI-compatible services, or a free model on your own computer with Ollama. Switch any time.

You approve every change

Every file edit shows a before and after preview first. Nothing is written until you say so.

Open source, yours to extend

MIT licensed with a plain-language guide to adding your own tools and features. Fork it and make it yours.

Up and running in a minute

  1. Open the app. Add your API key in Settings, or point it at Ollama on your own PC.
  2. Pick your project. Choose a folder, or import a public GitHub repo on your phone.
  3. Start talking. Tap the mic and say “Hey bro”. Type instead any time.

On a phone, open the app in Chrome and choose “Add to Home screen” to install it. The web version cannot run terminal commands; the desktop version can.
